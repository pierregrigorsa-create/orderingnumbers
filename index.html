🏠 Important reminder for Mum and Dad:
Please keep practising this concept with your child until they can order numbers confidently on their own. Here are easy ways to practise at home:

🪨 Stones or bottle tops — make 4 groups of objects. Ask: "Which group has the most?"
🖐️ Finger counting game — say a number, your child holds up that many fingers. Do all 4 numbers, then order them.
🃏 Number cards — write 0–9 on small pieces of paper. Pick 4. Order them both ways!

💡 If your child struggles, always go back to counting fingers first. Fingers make numbers real!
